# Code of conduct

Typecase is a small project, and we want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their background, experience or identity. That includes people who are new to open source, to Linux or to programming, and people whose first language is not English.

## What we expect

- Be friendly and patient, and assume good intentions.
- Respect other opinions, and criticize ideas rather than people.
- Accept it gracefully when a suggestion is declined. Everyone here gives their free time.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, and hostile or demeaning remarks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sexual language or images
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission

## Where this applies

This applies to everything around the project: issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere someone speaks on behalf of Typecase.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behavior bothers you, please write to [sascha@kleinod.studio](mailto:sascha@kleinod.studio). Reports are treated confidentially.

Comments may be edited or removed, and people who repeatedly or seriously break these rules may be blocked from the project.
